Sanket Mishra

Associate Professor | MIT Bengaluru, MAHE · Machine Learning & IoT Security

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Computer Engineering at Manipal Institute of Technology Bengaluru (MAHE). My research sits at the intersection of online machine learning, concept drift detection, deep learning, and Internet of Things security — building adaptive, scalable frameworks for real-time intrusion detection on evolving IoT data streams.

Previously an Assistant Professor at VIT Vellore (2020–2025), I earned my Ph.D. from BITS Pilani, Hyderabad. I have published 35+ peer-reviewed papers with 180+ citations in venues including Internet of Things, Cluster Computing, IEEE Access, and Scientific Reports.

I work on identifying key challenges in IoT ecosystems and solving them with frameworks that are adaptive, lightweight, and scalable. I am open to research collaborations, Ph.D./M.Tech supervision, and invited talks.

Research areas: Online machine learning · Concept drift · Deep learning · IoT intrusion detection · Lightweight edge ML

selected publications

  1. ML
    Attention-based bi-LSTM for anomaly detection on time-series data
    S. Mishra, V. Kshirsagar , R. Dwivedula , and 1 more author
    In International Conference on Artificial Neural Networks (ICANN) , 2021
    Abs

    Proposes an attention-based bidirectional LSTM model for detecting anomalies in IoT time-series data streams. The attention mechanism highlights critical temporal patterns that distinguish normal from anomalous behavior. Evaluated on real-world sensor datasets, the model achieves improved detection accuracy over baseline RNN and LSTM approaches.

  2. ML
    Tachyon: Enhancing stacked models using Bayesian optimization for intrusion detection using different sampling approaches
    T. A. Kumari , and S. Mishra
    Egyptian Informatics Journal, 2024
    Abs

    Introduces Tachyon, a stacked ensemble framework that uses Bayesian optimization to tune hyperparameters and select optimal sampling strategies for network intrusion detection. Compares SMOTE, ADASYN, and random undersampling on benchmark datasets. Results show that optimized stacking with appropriate sampling significantly improves F1-score on imbalanced attack traffic data.

  3. IoT
    LIRAD: Lightweight tree-based approaches on resource constrained IoT devices for attack detection
    S. Mishra, T. Anithakumari , R. Sahay , and 2 more authors
    Cluster Computing, 2025
    Abs

    Presents LIRAD, a family of lightweight tree-based classifiers designed for deployment on resource-constrained IoT edge devices. Addresses the challenge of running intrusion detection with limited memory and compute. Demonstrates that pruned decision trees and random forests can match deep learning accuracy while using a fraction of the resources.

  4. IoT
    Preventing data tampering in IoT networks
    R. K. Shrivastava , S. Mishra, V. E. Archana , and 1 more author
    In IEEE International Conference on Advanced Networks and Telecommunications Systems (ANTS) , 2019
    Abs

    Proposes a mechanism to detect and prevent data tampering attacks in IoT sensor networks. Uses cryptographic hashing combined with anomaly detection to verify data integrity at the edge before transmission to cloud backends. Evaluated on simulated IoT deployments with injected tampering attacks.

  5. IoT
    IoT-enabled early detection of diabetes diseases using deep learning and dimensionality reduction techniques
    T. Vaiyapuri , G. Alharbi , S. M. Dharmarajlu , and 2 more authors
    IEEE Access, 2024
    Abs

    Develops an IoT-based health monitoring system for early diabetes detection using deep learning on wearable sensor data. Applies PCA and feature selection to reduce dimensionality before feeding data into a neural network classifier. Achieves high sensitivity for pre-diabetic screening in clinical validation studies.

  6. ML
    An ingestion based analytics framework for complex event processing engine in internet of things
    S. Mishra, M. Jain , B. Siva Naga Sasank , and 1 more author
    In International Conference on Big Data Analytics (BDA) , 2018
    Abs

    Designs an ingestion pipeline that feeds heterogeneous IoT data streams into a Complex Event Processing (CEP) engine for real-time pattern detection. The framework handles schema evolution, late-arriving events, and high-throughput ingestion from thousands of concurrent sensors. Forms the foundational architecture for subsequent IoT security analytics work.

  7. IoT
    An evolutionary GA-based approach for community detection in IoT
    S. Mishra, C. Hota , L. Kumar , and 1 more author
    IEEE Access, 2019
    Abs

    Applies genetic algorithms to detect communities in IoT network topologies, enabling targeted security policies per device cluster. The evolutionary approach optimizes modularity and connectivity constraints simultaneously. Outperforms Louvain and label propagation on large-scale IoT network graphs.
