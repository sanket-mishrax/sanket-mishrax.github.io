## Biography

I am **Dr. Sanket Mishra**, an Associate Professor in the School of Computer Engineering at [Manipal Institute of Technology Bengaluru](https://www.manipal.edu/mu/campuses/mahe-bengaluru.html) (MAHE). My work focuses on building intelligent, adaptive systems for securing the Internet of Things — particularly through online machine learning, concept drift adaptation, and deep learning approaches.

I work at the intersection of online machine learning and big data, identifying key challenges in IoT ecosystems and solving them with frameworks that are adaptive, lightweight, and scalable. I enjoy creating visualizations and interactive tools to communicate research findings.
